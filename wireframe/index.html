Playlist‑to‑Email Campaign Automator

Turn any Spotify playlist into a ready-to-send Migma email campaign without leaving the client. Migma reads the playlist's metadata, derives an audience digest, proposes campaign angles, then streams a finished email into a live preview that pushes straight back to a Migma project.

Flow

Three steps, and their three names are the whole navigation model. A step is one surface that fills in as work finishes, not a screen that hands off to the next — which is why the seven mockups below sit inside three steps rather than seven. The rail under the header is the only way back.

Step 1 · Pick a playlist

  1. ·Mockups 02–04. One step, three things it can be showing — connect, choose, digest. Nothing hands off between them.
  2. ·You see the connect form on a first run only. After that: whichever playlist is open in Spotify right now, plus a searchable list of your own.
  3. ·You do one thing — pick a playlist. That click is the whole action; there is no separate Analyse button to press afterwards.
  4. ·Migma reads it through Spotify's own session, read-only, and the digest fills in under the name: tracks, runtime, artists, popularity, top genres, release era, audience tags.
  5. ·Change, beside the playlist name, swaps it. Anything read in the last 30 minutes comes back from cache, so a second look asks Spotify for nothing.

Step 2 · Choose & refine

  1. ·Mockups 05–06. Three tabs — Angles, Brief, Review — and the email preview stays beside them the whole time.
  2. ·You see three angles written from the digest. They are radio buttons, not checkboxes: one campaign per pass.
  3. ·You do the editing — tone, segment, subject, call to action. Review holds the comment thread for whoever else is on the campaign.
  4. ·Migma streams the email as it writes it, so the preview fills line by line instead of appearing at the end. Stop keeps what has arrived; Regenerate starts the pass over.
  5. ·Back is the rail. Step one is one click away, and the digest and the brief are both still there when you return.

Step 3 · Save & export

  1. ·Mockup 07. The step opens on a pre-flight read rather than on a bare Save button.
  2. ·You see the score first — conversion probability, the peak listening window, and which signals moved the number.
  3. ·You make two decisions — name a reviewer, then save the draft into the Migma project.
  4. ·Then it becomes a receipt. Copy HTML and Download .html are local: they never touch the workspace, and nothing is sent from here.
  5. ·Forward, not back. Create another returns to step one with the playlist still selected and its digest still cached.

The rail

  1. ·It is there from the first run, before a key is entered — three words are the clearest thing a cold start can say about what this does.
  2. ·It only goes backwards. A finished step is a button with a tick. The step you are on, and the ones ahead of it, are plain text rather than greyed-out buttons.
  3. ·Leaving a step cancels it. Whatever it had asked for is dropped, and a late reply cannot overwrite the newer step's own.
  4. ·Two things close it: a write in flight, and a saved draft. A receipt describes copy that has already left.

Read-only, through the client's own session via CosmosAsync — no separate OAuth prompt, no scopes to request.

CallFor
GET /v1/projectsproject picker
POST /v1/campaigns/ideasdigest → three angles
POST /v1/campaigns/generatebrief → streamed email

Every path and field name lives in one ENDPOINTS map, so aligning to the real schema is a few lines rather than a refactor.

Open questions

  1. 1 · Migma API shape. The four calls above are the plausible shape, not a documented one. Send me the real reference and I will align the client.
  2. 2 · Brand tokens. Palette, display face and the raised-button geometry are read out of migma.ai's own stylesheet and brand kit — nothing here is invented. If the internal design system has moved past the public build, one token block updates every screen.
  3. 3 · Segments. Shown as coming from Migma. If segments live elsewhere, that select becomes free text.
